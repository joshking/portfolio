## Priority Issues

### [P1] The hiring argument arrives too late

**Why it matters:** A Render reviewer must move through several routes before seeing infrastructure-specific evidence. "Product Designer / Design · Code · AI" could describe hundreds of candidates.

**Fix:** Keep the aurora signature, compress the first viewport, state the differentiator directly, and surface three immediate proofs: developer/infrastructure products, system-level product design, and hands-on implementation. Lead to StormForge with a visible interface artifact.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] The flagship case does not prove whole-system design

**Why it matters:** The role asks for multi-service IA, observability, coordinated deployment and rollback, first-run, and failure/recovery states. Optimize Live is relevant but presented primarily as a prose-led origin story, with key visual evidence sent to Figma.

**Fix:** Rebuild it as one six-to-eight-minute case: system and user model; operational complexity; pivotal decisions and tradeoffs; setup/first run; monitoring and recommendation states; safe application, degraded conditions, failure, and recovery; design-system-to-React implementation; cross-functional influence and outcomes. Use only genuine artifacts and states.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable shape`

### [P1] Writing defects weaken the quality claim

**Why it matters:** This role values obsessive craft and clear communication. Typos, duplicated resume bullets, vague leadership claims, and phrases like "standard design processes" create avoidable doubt.

**Fix:** Edit every visible sentence. Replace self-assessment with specific decisions, constraints, collaborators, and consequences. Correct spelling, duplicated bullets, awkward grammar, and stale dates.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P2] Reserve occupies the strongest visual position without evidence

**Why it matters:** The biggest feature on `/work` is inert and outranks the most relevant material while giving a recruiter nothing to evaluate.

**Fix:** Publish a credible case route, label it confidential/in progress and reduce it, or demote it below StormForge and Sauce Labs.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] The Lab is a catalog instead of an argument

**Why it matters:** Nine equal experiments hide the strongest proof. A reviewer should not have to discover that The Desk is the best density/operational-UI artifact.

**Fix:** Feature three experiments with a thumbnail and short facts for problem, authorship, implementation stack, and demonstrated capability. Nest remaining variants under one Network Fund collection.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`
